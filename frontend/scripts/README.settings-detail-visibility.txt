Settings detail visibility contract

The master dashboard display switches remain visible at the top of Settings.
Detailed configuration is progressively disclosed:
- Billing setup is hidden only when billing estimate, daily consumption and monthly consumption are all hidden.
- Spot settings and their current load/execution stack are hidden when Spot prices are hidden.
- Technology/FVE/Energy Flow details are hidden unless at least one of those modules is visible.
- The FVE technology row follows the Fotovoltaika switch independently.
- Topology, Site Capacity and phase settlement details follow the Energy Flow switch.

Hiding a detail section never deletes or changes its saved configuration.
